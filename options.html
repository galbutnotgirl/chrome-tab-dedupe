Tab Dedupe

When you open something that's already in a tab, the new tab closes and the one you already had moves next to the tab you're on.

Behavior

Only tabs that were just opened get redirected. Navigating a tab you're already working in is never touched, and a tab alone in its own window is left alone.

When you really do want a second copy

Tab jumped over to you and you actually wanted a fresh copy? Do it again within 8 seconds and the second one is left alone.

Related tabs

Right-click any tab → Close related tabs closes that whole excursion: the tab it was opened from, and everything opened from those. Chrome records which tab opened which, so this follows your actual trail rather than guessing by keyword.
