Per-site rules

Finished pages

Some tabs are done the moment they load — a Slack link that opened the app, a "you can close this tab" connector page, a Meet call that ended. The popup's Finished tab lists them and clears the lot in one click.

Cleanup

The popup's Clutter tab ranks tabs you're probably done with. It only proposes — you tick what closes, and Undo brings them back.
